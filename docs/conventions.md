# Conventions

- One memo, one thesis. Prefer `YYYY-MM-slug`.
- Separate facts, assumptions, and decisions.
- Ideas stay short until promoted to a memo.
- Papers and Quarto posts can share evidence from notebooks and datasets.
- Public essays live in `posts/<slug>/index.qmd` (Quarto). Do not use Substack.
- Import alphas from quant-trading; do not train them here.
- Full reports only for `alphas/<slug>` with `status: pass`. Prefer `YYYY-MM-slug` report names.
- Never commit brokerage credentials.
